How matching works

The prototype uses deterministic scoring so the judge can inspect the trade-offs. Pricing is intentionally not a user-fit signal.

Cold-start policy

New, verified astrologers receive a small exploration allowance instead of being excluded until they have history. They are never allowed to outrank a strong match purely because they are new.

Supply-side policy

Ranking is fit-first. The platform can reserve a controlled share of eligible impressions for verified new specialists, then expand or reduce that exposure based on real outcomes.

Unit-economics guardrail

The user-facing ranking should not simply route demand to the cheapest or highest-margin astrologer. Margin is a marketplace guardrail after fit, quality and user trust—not the primary ranking signal.
